Our History

A Decade of Fluid Dynamics Research

Founded in 2016, Stackly started as an research group at Bangalore’s leading science institute. We recognized that standard industrial filtration was treated as an unmonitored utility, resulting in massive liquid waste and chemical overheads. By merging high-density RO membranes with automated IoT telemetry, we created plug-and-play recovery grids that make water circularity cost-effective.

  • Modular structures easily adapted to space limits.
  • Custom-compiled membranes resistant to organic fouling.
  • Automated chemical dosing adjusted dynamically by IoT sensors.
