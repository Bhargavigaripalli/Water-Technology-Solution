How Our Filtration Process Works

Click through the stages of our industrial-grade purification grid to see how we refine raw wastewater into ultra-pure industrial water.

Sediment Filtration

Raw intake water is passed through high-density pleated media filters. This process filters out physical particulates including sand, silt, rust, and macro-debris down to 5 microns, protecting downstream membranes.

Activated Carbon Absorption

Water flows through porous activated carbon blocks that absorb volatile organic compounds (VOCs), chlorine, synthetic chemicals, pesticides, and unpleasant tastes or odors. An essential shield stage for our RO membranes.

Reverse Osmosis (RO)

The core chemical purification phase. Water is pressurized through semi-permeable spiral-wound RO membranes. This nanostructure blocks 99.9% of dissolved salts, heavy metals, microplastics, fluorides, and contaminants down to 0.0001 microns.

UV Sterilization

In the final sanitation loop, water passes through a chamber emitting high-intensity germicidal UV-C wavelengths (254nm). This destroys the cellular DNA of viruses, bacteria, and cysts, rendering water microbiologically pure without chemicals.
