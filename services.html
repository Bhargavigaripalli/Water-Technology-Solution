Industrial Reverse Osmosis (RO) Platforms

Our heavy-duty modular containerized RO skids are built to process highly saline wastewater, brackish water, or heavy chemicals. Each unit features composite polyamide membranes, high-efficiency pressure exchangers, and automatic anti-scaling dosing nodes to reduce waste by up to 98%.

  • Standard sizes from 50k to 5M liters per day
  • Up to 99.8% dissolved salts & chemical extraction
  • Low-energy recovery pumps lowering electricity draw by 30%

Smart Grid Telemetry & Sensor Dashboards

Bring full transparency to fluid processing. Our cloud telemetry gathers multi-point data across your piping infrastructure, feeding metrics to a real-time diagnostics dashboard.
